# Source Evaluation

## Evidence hierarchy

Use judgment rather than a rigid hierarchy, but generally prefer:

1. Primary research / original data
2. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses
3. High-quality academic syntheses
4. Reputable textbooks
5. Expert explanations
6. Popular summaries
7. Anecdotes / social media

## For every important claim ask

- What exactly is being claimed?
- What evidence supports it?
- How strong is the evidence?
- What would falsify it?
- Are there competing explanations?
- Is the effect causal or merely correlational?
- How large is the effect?
- Does it generalize?
- What uncertainty remains?

## Intellectual honesty

Mark claims as:
- **Established**
- **Well supported**
- **Plausible**
- **Contested**
- **Speculative**
- **Unknown**
