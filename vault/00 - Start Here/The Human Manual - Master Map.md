# The Human Manual — Master Map

## The architecture

```text
REALITY
  ↓
LIFE
  ↓
MIND
  ↓
THINKING
  ↓
SOCIETY
  ↓
LIVING WELL
  ↓
PRACTICE
```

## 1. Reality — What is the world like?

Physics, causality, information, probability, uncertainty, complexity, emergence.

## 2. Life — Why are living systems different?

Evolution, natural selection, adaptation, cooperation, competition, ecological dynamics.

## 3. Mind — What kind of creature is a human?

Predictive processing, active inference, perception, emotion, motivation, consciousness, language, creativity.

## 4. Thinking — How can we reason better?

Logic, epistemology, Bayesian reasoning, cognitive biases, decision theory, game theory, scientific reasoning.

## 5. Society — What happens when humans interact?

Economics, markets, incentives, institutions, inequality, norms, culture, coordination, collective action.

## 6. Living Well — What should we do with our lives?

Values, ethics, meaning, purpose, relationships, flourishing, wisdom, hope.

## 7. Practice — How do we actually live it?

Mindfulness, reflection, habits, decision journals, deliberate practice, feedback, experimentation.

## Cross-cutting principles

- Evolution
- Feedback loops
- Incentives
- Tradeoffs
- Adaptation
- Information
- Uncertainty
- Cooperation and competition
- Emergence
- Agency
- Constraints
- Selection effects
- Path dependence

## Key idea

The Manual should not merely tell people **what to believe**. It should teach them **how to investigate, reason, decide, and revise**.
